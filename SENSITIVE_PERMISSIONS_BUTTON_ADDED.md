### 🚀 **How It Works:**

1. **User grants basic permissions** (SMS, phone, notifications, etc.)
2. **"Continue to App"** button appears to proceed
3. **"Enable Full Protection"** button appears below with clear call-to-action
4. **Dialog opens** with two options:
   - **Accessibility Service** - Monitor messaging apps for scams
   - **Notification Access** - Read message notifications
5. **User selects option** → **Taken directly to settings page** with instructions
6. **User returns** → **Permissions automatically refresh**
